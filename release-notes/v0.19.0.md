This release aligns Android with Upscale Relay Server v0.19.0 and includes the
September playback and lifecycle audit fixes.

- Stop and reconnect wait for confirmed server cleanup. Canceled opens, native
  player commands, and Activity recreation retire their resources before a new
  session takes ownership.
- Initial playback and seeks share an inactivity timeout. Advancing subtitle
  indexing keeps the wait alive and displays progress, while stalled seeks can
  no longer wait indefinitely. Settings changes and reconnect preserve pause
  intent and track choices.
- Video-only sources avoid external audio attachment; subtitle-only sources use
  a single subtitle attachment. Confirmed muxed playback keeps audio and
  subtitles in the relay stream. Audio and subtitle selections remain distinct
  even when their numeric track IDs overlap.
- Local-file uplink, loopback streaming, and font downloads have bounded queues
  and stronger cancellation cleanup. Local HTTP requests and saved history are
  validated, and malformed bearer tokens cannot leak through header errors.
- Picture-in-Picture retains the playback Surface. Narrow layouts, control
  insets, scrubbing accessibility, and held pause-key behavior are improved.
- Android build tooling and dependencies are updated. Manual release builds
  use the exact requested tag.

The audit was validated with host tests, lint, and five device regressions on a
Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra, including live paused seeks, settings restarts, Activity
recreation, and local playback. Broader device and document-provider coverage
remains ongoing.
