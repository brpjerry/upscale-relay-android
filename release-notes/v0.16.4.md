Two more sources of stutter, both on resuming a video.

**Reopening a video at its saved position no longer drops frames.** The app
attaches the original file to the player for audio and subtitles, and it was
doing that twice — once for audio and once for subtitles, opening two separate
connections that each downloaded and searched the same file. The second one
took five seconds on a busy network, and the player was released to start
playing before the sound had actually caught up, so the picture ran on its own
and then had to reconcile. Resuming cost a dozen or more dropped frames.

There only ever needed to be one connection: it already carries the subtitles
too. Resuming now takes about a second and a half instead of six, and drops
nothing. As a side effect the audio and subtitle lists no longer show every
track twice.

**Returning from Picture-in-Picture or the background no longer stutters for
half a minute.** The video decoder cannot run without somewhere to draw, so
while the player is off screen the picture falls out of step with the sound —
almost 25 seconds adrift after 20 seconds away. The player has no way to jump
a live stream back into line, so it used to spend roughly as long as the gap
grinding its way back, dropping frames the whole time. The app now notices the
gap and restarts the stream at the point the audio had reached, so nothing you
were listening to is skipped and it is back in step in about a second.

Verified on a Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra: resuming a saved position drops no frames,
returning from 20 seconds in Picture-in-Picture comes back in step with none
either, and audio/video error stays in the tens of microseconds throughout —
including with a manual audio delay applied.

This release is signed with the same key as v0.15.0 through v0.16.3 and
upgrades over them normally. Moving from a locally built debug APK still
requires an uninstall, because debug and release signatures differ — export
your data first.
