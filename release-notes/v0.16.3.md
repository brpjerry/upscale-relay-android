Two fixes: the stutter at the start of every video, and a crash when you
seeked while paused.

**Video no longer stutters when a stream starts.** Making seeks fast in
v0.16.1 introduced this: the app now hands mpv the original file's audio and
subtitles once playback is already running, and for the second that takes, the
picture was running ahead on its own. When the sound finally arrived, the
player had to reconcile a second of drift all at once — a burst of dropped
frames, and the same thing again on every seek. The video now holds on its
first frame until the sound is in place, then both start together. A stream
start costs about one dropped frame, against roughly thirty before, and the
audio/video desynchronisation warning that accompanied every start is gone.
Seeks stay as fast as v0.16.1 made them.

**Seeking while paused no longer ends the session.** Pausing and then dragging
the seek bar put the session into a state the client considered impossible,
which tore it down and showed a "transition failed" error. A seek requested
while paused is perfectly ordinary — it now lands back in paused, with the new
position buffered and ready, and resumes where you left it.

Verified on a Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra: no dropped-frame accumulation across a
minute of playback with the buffer full, audio/video error in the tens of
microseconds, zero decoder drops, and pause → seek → resume returning to
normal playback with sound.

This release is signed with the same key as v0.15.0 through v0.16.2 and
upgrades over them normally. Moving from a locally built debug APK still
requires an uninstall, because debug and release signatures differ — export
your data first.
